    What Min3DStry Currently Is

    Maintainers: @admiraly, @Hxgg22, @Satixfied

    Min3DStry is a 1:1 port of upstream Mindustry (v8 build 160.8) for New Nintendo 3DS/2DS — not a simplified clone or demake. Upstream code and 3DS platform services (rendering, dual-screen UI/input, audio, and OS adapters) are modified where necessary for 3DS compatibility and performance while keeping the game technically original Mindustry: full content catalog, .msav maps/saves, and cross-platform multiplayer with the unmodified Java PC v160.8 release. Functional parity comes first, followed by tuning for 24+ FPS under high load on physical New Nintendo 3DS/2DS.

    Upstream v160.8 on ARM11

    The unified ARM11 build runs offline Ground Zero and joins unmodified Java v160.8 servers. Azahar verified fresh Ground Zero and Onset starts and stock saves in New 3DS mode. The audio-enabled Join path exercises original effects, building ambience and the Vorbis soundtrack.

    3DS Platform & Tooling

    Citro2D world/atlas renderer, libctru HID & touch palette, NDSP mixer + OGG Vorbis music streaming (libvorbisidec), Old/New 3DS probe, bounded allocator, New 3DS XL-verified parity harness (0x53a483c7) & 3dslink/SD deploy with benchmark-report, Flatpak/native Azahar harness (./dev.sh emu-test), and self-hosted NUMA CI runners.

    What’s Still Missing

    Offline audio, full campaign and logic interfaces, and remaining gameplay parity are still in development. The live audio replay verifies decoding and routing in Azahar. Physical listening and sustained ≥ 24 FPS gameplay under high load on physical New Nintendo 3DS/2DS remain open.
